FPGA · AI · NIT TRICHY

Adaptive CNN Accelerator

Explored an Adaptive Approximate Dynamic-Sparsity CNN Accelerator for energy-efficient FPGA edge inference. The work involved quantization, dynamic precision analysis, sparsity detection, zero-skipping, and depthwise separable convolution to reduce unnecessary computation. Implemented and evaluated the workflow using PyTorch and the Kria FPGA platform.

Kria FPGAPyTorchCNNVivadoZero-skipping

EMBEDDED · IoT · MONITORING

DISU Monitoring Prototype

Developed a divisional monitoring prototype for the Department of Posts to capture parcel movement, package weight, temperature, and security-related events. Integrated ESP32, Raspberry Pi, IR and ultrasonic sensors, PIR, DHT22, RTC, and a load cell with HX711, with backend data handling and monitoring.
